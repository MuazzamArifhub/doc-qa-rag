# Course policies (sample course)

This is a fictional course outline used to demonstrate the Q&A system.

## Assessment

- Weekly lab exercises: 20%
- Two assignments: 30%
- Midterm exam (week 7): 20%
- Final exam: 30%

You must pass the final exam to pass the course.

## Late submissions

Assignments lose 10% per day late, for up to three days. After three days they receive a mark of zero. Lab exercises cannot be submitted late, but your lowest two lab marks are dropped.

## Getting help

Office hours run Tuesdays and Thursdays from 2:00 to 3:30 pm. Teaching assistants also hold help sessions in the lab on Wednesday evenings. Post questions about course content on the discussion forum, but never post your assignment code.

## Academic integrity

You may discuss ideas with classmates, but the code you submit must be your own. If you use an AI assistant, say so in a comment at the top of your file and describe what it helped with.
